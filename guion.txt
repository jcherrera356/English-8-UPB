SLIDE 4 - SMALLER THAN ANTIOQUIA                         MEMBER 1
---------------------------------------------------------------------
To begin with (DM), let's imagine the size. El Orden Mundial reported
that (RS) historic Palestine covered only twenty-seven thousand five
hundred square kilometres, and that about fourteen million people
lived there. That is less than half of Antioquia.
In other words (DM), two peoples claim the same small piece of land.

Fuente: de Arguelles (2025), El Orden Mundial.

SLIDE 5 - WHY IT MATTERS TO US                           MEMBER 1
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Why should Colombians care?
[CLIC] Land. Like in Colombia, this conflict is about land and about
       people who are forced to leave their homes.
[CLIC] Peace deals. Israel and Palestine signed the Oslo Accords in
       1993; Colombia signed a peace agreement in 2016. However (DM),
       both cases show that a signature is not enough.
[CLIC] Fake news. Social media is full of opinions about Gaza. For this
       reason (DM), we decided to use only reliable sources.

SLIDE 6 - TIMELINE (8 clics, uno por ano)                MEMBER 2
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The BBC explained that (RS) the origins of this conflict went back
more than a century.

1917  Britain, which controlled Palestine, promised a "national home"
      for the Jewish people. An Arab majority already lived there.
[CLIC]
1947  After the Holocaust, the UN voted to divide the land into two
      states. Arab nations rejected the plan.
[CLIC]
1948  Israel declared independence. The BBC reported that (RS) about
      seven hundred and fifty thousand Palestinians had fled or had
      been forced from their homes. Palestinians call this the Nakba,
      the catastrophe.
[CLIC]
1967  In the Six-Day War, Israel took control of the West Bank, East
      Jerusalem and Gaza.
[CLIC]
1993  Israeli and Palestinian leaders signed the Oslo Accords, with the
      hope of two states.
[CLIC]
2005  Israel left Gaza. Two years later, Hamas took control.
[CLIC]
2023  On October 7, a Hamas-led attack killed about twelve hundred
      people in Israel and took two hundred and fifty-one hostages.
      Israel answered with a massive offensive in Gaza.
[CLIC]
2025  On October 10 a ceasefire began. Hamas returned the last living
      hostages and Israel freed almost two thousand prisoners.

Fuente: BBC News (2025), "History of the conflict explained".

SLIDE 8 - JERUSALEM (4 clics)                            MEMBER 2
---------------------------------------------------------------------
To understand the conflict, we watched a documentary by DW about
Jerusalem. It explained that (RS) the city was the heart of the
conflict.
[CLIC] Firstly (DM), Jerusalem is holy for three religions: Judaism,
       Christianity and Islam. The documentary said that (RS) almost
       two thirds of humanity had spiritual roots there.
[CLIC] Secondly (DM), in 1967 Israel annexed East Jerusalem. However
       (DM), the international community does not recognise it.
[CLIC] Thirdly (DM), thirty-nine percent of the people in Jerusalem
       are Palestinian. One Palestinian woman said that (RS) she did
       not have Israeli citizenship, only a residency permit.
[CLIC] Finally (DM), in 2000 the peace talks at Camp David failed
       because of Jerusalem. Arafat said that (RS) to accept that
       division he would need the signature of a billion Muslims.
       After that, the Second Intifada began.

Fuente: DW Documental, "Jerusalen - La eterna disputa por la Ciudad
Santa" (YouTube). Transcripcion en "que dice el video de youtube .txt".

SLIDE 10 - TWO SOLUTIONS                                  MEMBER 2
---------------------------------------------------------------------
There are two main frameworks.
[CLIC] Framework A: two states, one next to the other. It comes from
       Oslo. The BBC reported that (RS) more than one hundred and fifty
       countries recognised Palestine. However (DM), a poll found
       that (RS) sixty-three percent of Israelis rejected this idea.
[CLIC] Framework B: one shared state with equal rights for everyone.
       El Orden Mundial argued that (RS) the two-state solution was
       less and less viable. On the other hand (DM), Israel's 2018 law
       defines the country as a Jewish state.
Note: El Orden Mundial is an opinion article, so it shows one point of view.

Fuentes: BBC News (2025); de Arguelles (2025).

SLIDE 12 - THE HUMAN COST                                MEMBER 3
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Now, the current situation. DW reported that (RS) at least seventy-four
thousand six hundred people had died in Gaza since 2023, and that
almost one hundred and seventy-six thousand had been injured, according
to Gaza's health authorities. The UN considers these figures reliable.
Moreover (DM), more than one thousand six hundred people had died after

In addition (DM), the BBC reported that (RS) more than three hundred

---------------------------------------------------------------------

the two point one million people in Gaza have been displaced again and
again, and reconstruction has not started.
